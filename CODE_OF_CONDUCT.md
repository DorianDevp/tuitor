# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is
best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others'
private information are not acceptable, in the project's spaces or when representing it.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@DorianDevp](https://github.com/DorianDevp), through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/DorianDevp/tuitor/security/advisories/new) if it must
stay confidential, or by opening an issue otherwise. Reports are handled promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that break this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently, following the
Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
